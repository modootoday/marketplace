This is a synthetic product-research exercise dated 2026-10-01. We are considering an appointment-reminder product, not implementing billing. Do not contact anyone, publish a page, run a purchase test or use external services. Local skill guidance may be read.

Our supplied interview notes:

- Six independent one-location shop owners currently use a free calendar and manual reminder messages. All six said that workflow is adequate for their current needs. None was shown a price or made a purchase commitment.
- Four coordinators each manage five locations. They currently use the same free calendar plus a shared spreadsheet to coordinate reminder schedules across locations. Each reported recurring coordination effort and asked about a shared overview and location-specific permissions. No one stated a monetary value for that effort, an amount they would pay or a purchase commitment.

A teammate proposes 9,900 won per account per month with up to twenty locations, the same feature set for every account, and a 20% introductory discount. They argue that the discount will make the one-location owners buy and that larger customers can get the same plan without further research.

We can spend at most three staff hours over the next five days on a small research test involving these ten contacts. There is no supplied cost model, competitor price study or actual checkout.

Should we keep, change or hold this pricing idea, and what should our next small test be? Explain the reasoning and how its result would change the decision.
