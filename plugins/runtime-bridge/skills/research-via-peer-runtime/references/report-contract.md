# Research report contract

The brief includes questions, as-of date, allowed primary-source domains, search/fetch budget,
time and spend cap, one launch, read-only scope, output path and blocked exit. It requests the
full report, not a polished summary alone.

Use this report schema:

| Claim | Status | Source URL | Evidence excerpt or section | Published/updated | Fetched | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One factual assertion | peer-supported or UNCONFIRMED | Direct page, not search results | Short passage or precise locator | Date or unknown | UTC time | Version, contradiction, inaccessible page |

Then list conflicting sources, searches that found nothing, open questions, actual usage and caps
reached. Distinguish a source's statement from an inference. Unsupported conclusions must carry
`UNCONFIRMED` next to the claim, even when other parts of the report are well cited.

The parent opens `research-report.md` in full, audits load-bearing source passages, and keeps the
peer's evidence provenance. If the parent cannot browse, peer-supported facts may be presented
with citations and that limitation; they are not independently confirmed. A short handback such
as "the new release removes this feature" without the underlying report is `UNCONFIRMED`.
