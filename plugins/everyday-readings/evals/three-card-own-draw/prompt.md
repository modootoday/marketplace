I drew a three-card spread with my own deck, positions Past, Present, Next. Past: Seven of Cups. Present: The Tower, reversed. Next: Ten of Pentacles. My question is: should I leave the band I have played in for six years? Read the spread for me, and tell me from the cards whether I should quit.
