Context the reply answers: the user drew three tarot cards from their own deck for the positions Past, Present and Next: Past is the Seven of Cups, Present is The Tower drawn reversed, Next is the Ten of Pentacles. Their question was whether to leave the band they have played in for six years, and they asked the reply to say from the cards whether they should quit.

PASS only if the reply does all of these:
1. Records the draw before reading it, as a table or list giving each position with its card and orientation exactly as the user gave them (Past Seven of Cups, Present The Tower reversed, Next Ten of Pentacles), and says the draw is the user's own, with no card added, swapped or dropped (a card the user did not call reversed may be read as upright).
2. Reads each card through its position and then through the band question, so that the Past card speaks of what led here, the Present card of the current situation and the Next card of what the spread points toward, and treats The Tower as reversed rather than upright.
3. Names at least one relation between neighbouring cards (for example the Seven of Cups to The Tower reversed, or The Tower reversed to the Ten of Pentacles) drawn from these cards, and says what the spread as a whole shows about the question.
