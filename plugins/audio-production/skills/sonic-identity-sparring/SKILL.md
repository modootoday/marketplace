# Sonic identity sparring

"Upbeat and modern" describes every brand's music brief. Directions have to
differ enough that picking one is a decision.

## Questions that change the answer

Ask only what is missing: where people hear the brand (app sounds, shop speakers,
short videos, phone hold), how long they hear it each time, what feeling the
brand must not give, and what competitors sound like.

## Two or three directions

For each:

- A name and one sentence of what it says about the brand.
- Concrete choices: instruments or sound sources, tempo range, key or mood,
  whether there is a voice and what kind, and a 1-3 second logo sound idea.
- Where it works and where it fails (a calm direction disappears in a noisy shop;
  a bright one tires people who hear it all day).
- What it costs: library music, commissioned, generated with a licence that
  covers commercial use.

## One test

The cheapest test that tells: play two 10-second sketches to five customers in
the real setting and ask what kind of business it sounds like. Do not declare a
direction proven before that.
