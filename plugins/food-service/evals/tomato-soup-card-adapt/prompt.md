Recipe: Tomato soup, serves 4. 2 tbsp butter, 1 yellow onion (diced), 3 garlic cloves, 1 can (800 g) whole peeled tomatoes, 500 ml vegetable stock, 120 ml heavy cream, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt. Melt butter at medium heat, cook onion 8 minutes, add garlic 1 minute, add tomatoes and stock, simmer 25 minutes, blend, stir in cream and sugar and salt.

Adaptation: I have no heavy cream, and I want to make it in a slow cooker.
