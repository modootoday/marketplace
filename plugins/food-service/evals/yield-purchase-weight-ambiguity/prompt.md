Our recipe card serves 6 and lists carrots 1.2 kg and butter 120 g. Peeling and trimming loses 10% of carrots as purchased. We need 72 portions. Carrots cost 2100 KRW per kg; butter is sold only in whole 250 g blocks at 3000 KRW each.

Prepare a purchasing worksheet for the chef to approve, with checkable unit conversions including butter grams, kilograms and blocks. Separate ingredient usage cost from the amount paid for whole packs. Do not silently fill in a preparation-weight label that is absent from the card; identify anything that must be confirmed before approval.
