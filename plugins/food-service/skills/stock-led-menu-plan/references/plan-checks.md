# Plan checks

Read this before writing the final answer.

## Dates

Write each day with its weekday computed from the date the user gave (if today is Monday
5 Oct, then 7 Oct is Wednesday and 8 Oct is Thursday). Copy every use-by date exactly as
supplied; never restate a date the user did not give. Do not add facts about stock
(thawed, boneless, opened) beyond what was supplied; state them as assumptions.

## Tables the answer must contain

1. Stock sorted by date, as supplied.
2. Menu: day, dish, stock used with amounts.
3. Balance: one row per stocked item, columns start, then left after each day.
4. Gaps: item, needed, on hand, shortfall (0 allowed), first day needed. Every item that the
   menu uses appears, so the reader can check needed minus on hand.
5. Storage and feasibility, as checks the cook runs (fridge space on the heaviest day,
   freezer and thaw time, cooking time and equipment, diet constraint).
6. Assumptions: portion per eater by item, and anything not supplied.

## Closing lines

- Items at or near their date were planned first; the cook decides from the label and
  their own checks whether they are fit to eat. The plan makes no statement of safety.
- The cook confirms use-by dates, amounts, storage and cooking fit before shopping or
  cooking.
