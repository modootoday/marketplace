# Card and adaptation log

## Base card rules

- Copy the source wording of each ingredient line and each step. Do not paraphrase,
  reorder, merge or split steps, and do not reword quantities (keep "1 can (800 g)").
- Number of ingredient lines and steps on the card equals the source.
- Add no heading text that carries cooking advice. Notes go below the card.
- A "Not stated in the source" line is written only for a value the method needs and the
  source lacks (no oven temperature, no time for a step). If every value the method needs is
  present, write no such line and do not list things the cook might want to know.

## Appliance rules of thumb (all estimates; state the rule used and label every result)

| Move to | Rule | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slow cooker | stovetop or oven time of 15 to 30 minutes becomes 4 to 6 hours low or 1.5 to 2.5 hours high; 35 to 45 minutes becomes 6 to 10 low or 3 to 4 high; cut added liquid by about a quarter because little evaporates | food soft, meat and poultry at a safe internal temperature, taste for thinness |
| Air fryer | oven temperature minus about 20 C (25 F), time minus about 20 percent, single layer, turn halfway | colour and crispness, internal temperature for meat |
| Pressure cooker | about one third of the simmer or braise time plus time to come to pressure, natural release for soups | tenderness, then reduce on the stove if thin |

Do not quote a computed figure you then discard (a rejected "2x" time): every number in the
reply is either a source number or a labelled estimate.

## Worked example (pancakes, swap egg, move to a pan on an induction hob)

Source: "Pancakes, serves 2. 120 g flour, 1 egg, 200 ml milk, 1 tsp baking powder, pinch of
salt. Whisk, rest 10 minutes, fry in butter 2 minutes per side."

Base card: the same five ingredient lines and the same three step phrases, nothing else.

Adapted numbers are labelled estimate; the base card numbers are labelled source. The adapted
