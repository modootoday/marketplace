PASS only if all three hold:
1. It offers two or three directions that differ in more than colour (for example photo versus
   type-led versus illustration, or different composition), and at least two of them name
   concrete choices: colours (named or hex), a typeface, and an image style.
2. Each direction has a stated downside or risk (who it misses, what it might look like, what it
   costs to make).
3. It ends with which direction or pair to try first and a cheap way to test it, rather than
   declaring a winner as proven.

FAIL if it gives only adjectives, a single direction, or no downsides.
