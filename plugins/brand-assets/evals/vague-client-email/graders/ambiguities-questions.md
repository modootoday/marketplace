Context the reply answers: a designer pasted a client email from Mina of Daon Tea House: "we need something for the autumn sale, like last time but fresher. Our logo is attached. Can you make it pop and have it ready for the weekend? Also put the discount on it." The designer does not remember what was made last time and has no brand guide. The ambiguities in the email include the deliverable and format (flyer, social post, poster, size), what "last time" was, what "fresher" and "pop" mean, which weekend and deadline, the discount amount and terms, the copy, and the brand assets beyond the logo.

PASS only if the reply does all of these:
1. Lists the ambiguities found in the email (at least the deliverable or format, what "like last time" refers to, the discount details, and the deadline).
2. Asks the client at most four questions, prioritised, and turns the lower-ranked ambiguities into stated assumptions instead of more questions.
3. Gives each question a default the client can confirm in a word, framed as a proposal.
4. Starts no design work and invents no facts about the client (no discount value, size or brand colour presented as known).
