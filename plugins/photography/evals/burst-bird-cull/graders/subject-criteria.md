The photographer also shoots weddings with the app and asks how to set the cull up so it can be trusted for the burst and for events. No image pixels were supplied, only the report.

PASS only if the reply does all of these:
1. Does not tell the photographer to delete the rejects: it recommends ranking and flagging only, keeping every original, and having the photographer review all frames including the rejected ones.
2. Sets criteria for the subject instead of generic ones: for a bird in flight it names criteria such as eye sharpness, wing position or head angle, and points out that a closed eye on a bird may be the nictitating membrane (normal anatomy), so the three "closed eyes" rejects cannot be trusted as a defect without looking.
