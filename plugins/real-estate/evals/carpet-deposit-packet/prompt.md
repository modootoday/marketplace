Synthetic example, made-up names. I am the property manager for a flat. Please arrange a claim packet for the deposit dispute with the former tenant, Ms R. These are all the records I have:

- Lease (doc L1): signed 1 March 2019 for 12 months then monthly; deposit 1,500; rent 1,200 a month. Clause 9: tenant pays for damage beyond ordinary wear.
- Move-in report (doc M1), dated 1 March 2019: living room carpet "good"; walls "good"; no photos.
- Move-out report (doc M2), dated 20 August 2026 (the tenant handed back the keys that day): living room carpet "stained"; walls "good"; 4 photos (docs P1 to P4), the stained carpet is in P2 and P3.
- Invoice (doc I1), 28 August 2026: replacement of living room carpet, 480. The invoice does not say the age of the old carpet; the carpet was installed before 2019 according to my note, so it was at least 7 years old.
- Today is 29 September 2026. I have not sent the tenant an itemised statement or returned any of the deposit.

I know my area has deadlines for returning a deposit but I do not have the rule in front of me. Please build the packet.
