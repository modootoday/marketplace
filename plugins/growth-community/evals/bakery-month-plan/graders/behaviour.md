PASS only if the plan does all of these: a small set of recurring themes or pillars; a posting cadence that fits about three hours a week (roughly two to four posts a week, not daily); a dated calendar with a format and a call to action per post; at least one post a week that pushes the whole-cake pre-order with a concrete next step; and something to measure each week beyond likes (saves, messages, orders). FAIL if it plans a post every day, has no dates, or never asks for the pre-order.
