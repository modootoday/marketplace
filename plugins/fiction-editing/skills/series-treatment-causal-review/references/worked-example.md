# Worked example: six-episode outline

Synthetic source, pasted by the writer, with a limit of about 50 words:

Ep1: Odile, a relief lighthouse keeper, is moved to a mainland desk after reporting a flooded
generator room. Ep2: to prove the flood warning was ignored, Odile copies the tide logs at
night. Ep3: the copies show two pages missing (Benet, her supervisor, keeps bees on the rock and
talks about them at length). Ep4: Odile confronts Benet. Ep5: Benet resigns. Ep6: Odile is
offered the island post back.

## Extraction table

| Ep | Event | Cause | Choice | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Moved to a mainland desk | Her report of the flooded generator room | not on the page (the text says she reports, not why) | Off the island |
| 2 | Copies the tide logs at night | Wants to prove the warning was ignored | Copy the logs | Two pages missing from the copies (Ep3) |
| 3 | The copies show two pages missing | The copying (Ep2) | not on the page | Missing pages are known |
| 4 | Confronts Benet | Missing pages (Ep3) as the nearest event; the text does not say Benet removed them | Confront | Benet resigns (Ep5) |
| 5 | Benet resigns | The confrontation (Ep4) | Benet's own choice | Post is open (Ep6) |
| 6 | Offered the island post back | Benet's resignation (Ep5) | not on the page | End of the outline |

What the table shows about the method:

- A consequence cell is read off the next episode's event or cause. The Ep2 consequence is
  the Ep3 pages; it is not left blank and not replaced by an invented object.
- Where the text gives no link between two neighbours (Ep3 pages and Ep4 Benet), the cause
  cell names the nearest event and says the link is not stated. No cell says Benet took the
  pages.
- `not on the page` is written only for a cell with nothing to read off: here the Ep1, Ep3
  and Ep6 choices.

## Treatment, 52 words (limit about 50)

Odile, a relief keeper, is moved to a mainland desk after reporting a flooded generator room.
To prove the flood warning was ignored, she copies the tide logs at night. The copies show two
missing pages. Odile then confronts her supervisor Benet. Benet resigns, and Odile is offered
the island post back.

"To prove" and "after" carry links the text states. "then" marks the Ep3 to Ep4 step where
the text states none; "so" would assert a relation the writer did not give.

## Missing motivation, as questions for the writer

1. Ep1: why does Odile report the flood, and what does it cost her beyond the move? The text
   gives the report and the move only.
2. Ep4: what makes Odile confront Benet after the pages are found? The text does not say
   whether she suspects him.
3. Ep6: does Odile want the island post? The offer is on the page, her answer is not.

Each question rests on a missing cell in the table, not on an assumed fact about the story.
