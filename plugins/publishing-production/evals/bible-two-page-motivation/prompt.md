Ep3: a parcel turns out to hold ledgers from the harbour office. Ep4: Mara hides the ledgers and lies to her brother. Ep5: Mara goes to the harbour master with the ledgers. Compress to two pages' worth of treatment (about 150 words here) and show where the motivation for her change is missing.
