My biology exam is in 14 days and today is day 0. Turn my notes into flashcards and a review schedule. Make a card for everything, I want to be thorough.

Notes:
1. Mitochondria are the organelles where most cellular ATP is made by aerobic respiration.
2. They have a double membrane: a smooth outer membrane and a folded inner membrane.
3. The folds of the inner membrane are called cristae, and they increase the surface area for ATP production.
4. The fluid-filled space inside the inner membrane is the matrix, where the Krebs cycle takes place.
5. The electron transport chain sits on the inner membrane and pumps protons into the space between the two membranes.
6. Protons flow back through ATP synthase, and this flow drives the making of ATP from ADP and phosphate.
7. Mitochondria contain their own circular DNA and ribosomes, which supports the idea that they descend from free-living bacteria.
8. Our teacher mentioned that the word "mitochondrion" was coined in 1898 by Carl Benda.
9. Cells with high energy demand, such as muscle cells, contain more mitochondria.
10. The overall equation is glucose plus oxygen giving carbon dioxide, water and ATP.
