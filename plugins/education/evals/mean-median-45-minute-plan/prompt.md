Please draft a 45-minute lesson plan for my grade 7 math class of 24 students.

Objectives: (1) students calculate the mean and median of a set of at most 9 numbers; (2) students explain which of the two better describes a data set that has one extreme value.

Supports I need built in: one student needs large-print materials, and three multilingual learners need vocabulary support in simplified English.

Constraints: total teacher talk must not exceed 12 minutes, and the last 5 minutes must be an exit ticket.
