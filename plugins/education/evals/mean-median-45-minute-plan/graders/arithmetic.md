Context the reply answers: a teacher asked for a 45-minute plan for a grade 7 math class of 24. Objectives: (1) calculate the mean and median of at most 9 numbers; (2) explain which of mean and median better describes a data set with one extreme value. Supports: one student needs large-print materials; three multilingual learners need vocabulary support in simplified English. Constraints: total teacher talk at most 12 minutes, and the last 5 minutes are an exit ticket.

PASS only if the reply does all of these:
1. Presents an activity table with minutes per activity (or start-end minutes) and shows the sum equal to exactly 45, written out as a total next to the period length.
2. Makes the last 5 minutes an exit ticket, as the fixed slot in the table.
3. Shows the teacher-talk minutes added up (the activities where the teacher is talking) and a total of at most 12, compared against the cap of 12.
4. Includes opening, transitions or a close as timed items rather than only core activities, so that the time is not left unaccounted for.
