Context the reply answers: a teacher asked for a 45-minute grade 7 math lesson plan (24 students) with objective 1 (calculate the mean and median of at most 9 numbers) and objective 2 (explain which of mean and median better describes a data set with one extreme value). Supports needed: one student needs large-print materials; three multilingual learners need vocabulary support in simplified English. Constraints: teacher talk at most 12 minutes; the last 5 minutes an exit ticket.

PASS only if the reply does all of these:
1. Maps each activity to objective 1 or objective 2 (by number), or labels it routine (opening, transition), and every objective has at least one activity where students show it.
2. Names a concrete accommodation for the large-print need and places it in a named activity or minute range (for example large-print worksheet in the practice block), and does the same for the vocabulary support for the multilingual learners (for example a simplified-English word bank for "mean", "median", "extreme value"), not just a general statement of support.
3. Lists materials grouped by activity or in one list, including the large-print copies and the vocabulary support materials, with a quantity for the class of 24 where it states one.
4. Does not label or diagnose any student beyond the needs given, and says what it could not know (prior knowledge, room layout) or that the teacher adapts the plan.
