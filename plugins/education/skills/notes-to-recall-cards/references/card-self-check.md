# Card self-check with a worked example

Run this on every card before the deck is shown. Example notes are about the heart, not the user's topic.

Notes:

1. The right atrium receives oxygen-poor blood from the body and the right ventricle sends it to the lungs.
2. Valves keep blood flowing one way, and the valve between the left atrium and left ventricle is the mitral valve.
3. The sinoatrial node sets the heart rate, and it sits in the wall of the right atrium.

## Three tests per card

1. Wording test: no key word of the back may appear in the front. A front "What flow drives the pump?" with
   the back "The pump's flow" fails. Rewrite the front to ask for the missing piece.
2. One-item test: the front names at most one thing to recall, and the back is one fact. A back that joins two
   items with "and" is two cards or one of the items. A front that lists two items to explain ("what do the
   DNA and the ribosomes show") is rewritten around one item.
3. Same-line test: two cards from one note line are allowed only when each fact can be recalled without the
   other. Two cards about the same structure (its name and its purpose) are near-duplicates: keep one.

## Worked result

| # | Front | Back | Line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Which chamber receives oxygen-poor blood from the body? | Right atrium | 1 |
| 2 | Which valve lies between the left atrium and left ventricle? | Mitral valve | 2 |
| 3 | Which structure sets the heart rate? | Sinoatrial node | 3 |

Pick the load-bearing fact of each multi-fact line: the function or mechanism, not the label. For line 3 a
better card is "What sets the heart rate?" than "Where is the sinoatrial node?". Rank cards that test a
mechanism (what drives or sets what) above cards that test a name or a place.

Line 1 holds two facts (receiving, sending to the lungs): only the first is carded, the second is listed as
left for a second batch. Line 2 holds two facts too (one-way flow, the mitral valve): one card, the other left
for the second batch. Line 3's location is a second fact left for the second batch. Not allowed: "What does the
sinoatrial node do and where is it?" (two parts), and a back such as "The right atrium and right ventricle"
(two items).

A line that holds several facts yields one card per pass; the extra facts are named in the second-batch offer, not
added as more cards from the same line.
