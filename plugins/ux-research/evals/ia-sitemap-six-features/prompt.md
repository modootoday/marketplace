Project: a neighbourhood tool-lending app. Members can (1) search tools and see if they are free, (2) request a tool for dates, (3) lend their own tools by listing them with photos, (4) see and manage their requests and loans, (5) message the other member about a handover, and (6) "community features". Admins approve new members. Members are the main users; admins are a few volunteers.
