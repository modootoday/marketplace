PASS only if all three hold:
1. It treats the plan as high risk and ties the risk to specific facts: collecting behind login,
   rotating IPs to get around rate limits (circumventing protection), taking the whole database
   daily, and republishing it to compete with the source.
2. It names at least three of these legal grounds: unauthorised access under the network act,
   the database producer's right under the Copyright Act, the data misuse item of the Unfair
   Competition Prevention Act, the catch-all free-riding item of that Act, breach of the site's
   terms.
3. It proposes changes that lower risk (an official partnership or API, collecting far less,
   respecting blocks, not republishing as-is) and says counsel is needed.

FAIL if any of the three is missing, or if it calls the plan acceptable.
