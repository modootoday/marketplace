PASS if the draft covers purposes, items, retention, entrustment to processors (payment and email), an overseas transfer section for the US email provider with its details, data subject rights and a privacy officer, and leaves company-specific facts the user did not give (company and service names, officer name and contact, the email provider's identity) as placeholders. Standard or statutory retention terms (until account deletion, legally required retention of payment records) are acceptable and do not count as invented facts. FAIL if it omits the overseas transfer or invents a company name, officer or provider.
