PASS only if the reply explains that data needed to perform the order contract (name, contact,
delivery address) can be collected without consent on the contract-performance basis listed in
the Act, so extra mandatory consent boxes are not the safe direction, and that the shop still has
to disclose the processing (for example in the privacy policy). FAIL if it endorses adding more
consent as the fix without mentioning the contract basis.
