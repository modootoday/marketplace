Two dedicated carriers bid on our DC1 to Store lane. Which is cheaper?

Bid A: 4,200 per week fixed plus 1.35 per mile. Bid B: no fixed charge, 2.40 per mile. The lane is 310 miles one way, 5 trips per week, one delivery stop per trip, and the truck returns empty on every trip. Neither bid says anything about fuel or other fees.
