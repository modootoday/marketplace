An AI wrote this single crochet amigurumi giraffe pattern for me. I asked for a giraffe with a body, a neck, a head, two ossicones (the little horns), four legs and a tail. Can I stitch it as written? Check it and fix it.

Abbreviations: sc single crochet, inc 2 sc in one stitch, dec sc2tog, MR magic ring. All parts are worked in continuous rounds.

BODY
R1: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: inc x6 (12)
R3: (sc, inc) x6 (18)
R4: (2 sc, inc) x6 (24)
R5-R7: sc around (24)
R8: (2 sc, dec) x6 (20)
R9: (sc, dec) x6 (12)
Stuff the body and leave the 12 stitches open.

NECK
Join yarn at the body opening. R1-R12: sc around (12). Stuff.

HEAD
R1: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: inc x6 (12)
R3: (sc, inc) x6 (18)
R4: (2 sc, inc) x6 (28)
R5-R6: sc around (24)
R7: (2 sc, dec) x6 (18)
R8: (sc, dec) x6 (12)
Stuff. Fasten off.

OSSICONES (make 2)
R1: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2-R4: sc around (6)
Fasten off.

LEGS (make 4)
R1: 6 sc in MR (6)
R2: inc x6 (12)
R3-R8: sc around (12)
Stuff. Fasten off.

TAIL
Ch 10, sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across (10). Fasten off.
