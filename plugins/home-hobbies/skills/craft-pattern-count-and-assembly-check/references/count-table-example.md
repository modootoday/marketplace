# Count table and verified paragraph: worked example

Read this before writing the check or a corrected pattern.

## Count table

Each repeat is written as stitches consumed per repeat and stitches made per repeat. A
"dec" consumes 2 and makes 1; an "inc" consumes 1 and makes 2; a plain sc consumes 1 and
makes 1.

| Round | Repeat | Consumed | Made | Total after | Stated | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R7 | sc around | 24 | 24 | 24 | 24 | ok |
| R8 | (2 sc, dec) x6 | 4 x 6 = 24 | 3 x 6 = 18 | 18 | 20 | wrong, 18 |
| R9 | (sc, dec) x6 | 3 x 6 = 18 | 2 x 6 = 12 | 12 | 12 | ok |

Consumed must equal the previous total. If it does not, the repeat or the previous count is
wrong; say which, and give the number the repeat produces.

A foundation chain of N, worked from the second chain from the hook, gives N - 1 stitches.
