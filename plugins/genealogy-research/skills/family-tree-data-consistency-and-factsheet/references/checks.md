# Consistency checks

| Rule | Flag when |
| --- | --- |
| Parent age | parent-child birth gap under 15 years, or mother over 50 at the birth |
| Order | death year earlier than birth year of the same person |
| Event after death | marriage, birth of a child or burial-independent event after the death year (a child born up to nine months after a father's death is possible) |
| Spouse | marriage year before either spouse's birth, or a death before the marriage |
| Duplicates | same name and similar birth under two identifiers: list as possible duplicate, do not merge |

Synthetic example. Export A: I1 Sam Orr b.1860; I2 Eve Orr b.1851, mother of I1; I3 Kay Orr b.1858, wife of I1, d.1840. Export B: I1 Sam Orr b.1861.

- I2 and I1: 1860 - 1851 = 9 years, implausible, check both birth years.
- I3: death 1840 is before birth 1858 (1840 - 1858 = -18), check; also before the marriage to I1 if one is recorded.
- I1 differs between exports: birth 1860 (A) and 1861 (B). Listed, not resolved. Consult the source of each export.

Fact sheet for I1: Identity: I1, Sam Orr. Direct evidence: "born 1860" per Export A, "born 1861" per Export B. Interpretation: none supported yet. Open questions: which birth year does a primary record give; who is the mother.
