Synthetic example. I am researching Henrik Lund, born about 1851, and want to find his baptism, his parents and his early land. Where should I search next?

What I have: the 1880 census puts him in the hamlet of Oakvale, Ridgemont Township, Tallow County. The Catholic church St. Brendan is in Millbrook, 4 km away across the county line in Dunmore County. Tallow County was created in 1873 from the eastern part of Dunmore County. St. Brendan belonged to the Diocese of Harrow until 1889, when the Diocese of Ellis was created from part of it.

Searches done: the 1880 census (found him) and the Tallow County deed index from 1873 on (nothing under Lund).

An AI told me: "Search the Tallow County Catholic registers at the Ellis Diocesan Archive; baptism entries there list the parents' names."

Finding aid excerpts I copied:
- Harrow Diocesan Archive: sacramental registers, St. Brendan Millbrook, vols. 1-4, 1848-1889. Originals only, onsite reading room. Vol. 2 (1855-1861) water damaged. No index.
- Ellis Diocesan Archive: sacramental registers 1889-1950, all parishes of the Ellis diocese. Online: name and date index only; images onsite.
- Dunmore County Courthouse, Register of Deeds: land deeds 1840-1873, indexed by grantor and grantee, copies by mail.

Make me a search plan I can follow.
