Prepare a supplied-data resale contribution calculation and a cash-timing bridge. Do not perform account actions or contact anyone.

Currency CAD, campaign R2. I buy and resell: I bought 100 units for CAD20 each, sold all 100 at CAD50 each, and refunded 10 buyers CAD50 each. The agreement leaves the refunded units' inventory cost with me; they were damaged and disposed of, so all ten units have documented unrecoverable cost and no resale value, supplier credit or return proceeds. I bear shipping CAD300 for the batch and a single processor fee CAD90. Gross retained customer receipts are CAD4500. The processor's net receivable CAD4410 already deducts CAD90; CAD1000 of that receivable is temporarily held and CAD3410 has been paid. No loss determination exists for the hold. No other variable costs are supplied. For a separate time-cost scenario, I spent 10 hours at my supplied opportunity rate CAD25/hour. Taxes are unknown. Show operating contribution, retained-sales margin, cash timing and the separate labor scenario.
