# Video scene structure

HOOK: one opening line within about three seconds. BODY: one message per scene. CTA: a closing action. Three scenes can cover those roles; five to eight often use one opening, three to six middle scenes, and one close. Adapt the roles to the topic.

Subtitle guides: HOOK about 20 characters, BODY 40, CTA 20. Tool limits do not enforce these; split sentences that cannot be read in one breath. Duration is estimated from subtitle length.

Use one or two transition styles (fade is a restrained default). Avoid repetitive motion or alternating opposing zooms. Motion can be stronger at opening/close and quieter during explanations.

Canvas sizes: vertical 9:16 at 1080x1920; horizontal 16:9 at 1920x1080; square 1:1 at 1080x1080. Both dimensions must be even for encoding. Set the ratio before adding scenes; resizing an existing timeline affects all scenes.
