# CTA wording principles

- Clarity: one verb and one action; explain the destination in the wording.
- Specificity: state the real benefit or time required only if supplied or confirmed. A quotation taking three minutes or free first-order delivery cannot be invented.
- Less friction: mention a short process or guarantee only when it is actually offered.
- Preserve the source's formality and tone; informational text should stay calm.
- Use actual search wording only after checking it. That is a writing choice, not a measured effectiveness claim.
- Do not invent urgency, scarcity, reviews, buyers, satisfaction, or demographic stereotypes.
