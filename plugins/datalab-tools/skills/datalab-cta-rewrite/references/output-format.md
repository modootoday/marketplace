# CTA output

Quote the original CTA, or say none was supplied. Provide two or three copyable candidates, each with its chosen technique (clarity, specificity, less friction, or confirmed search wording) and one sentence explaining why.

Do not include expected conversion/click/lift rates, A/B winners, ROI or sales forecasts, invented customer/review counts, optimal posting times, or claims that a color gets more clicks. Those outcomes were not measured.
