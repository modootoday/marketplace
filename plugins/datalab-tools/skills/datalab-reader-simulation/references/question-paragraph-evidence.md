# Question-to-paragraph evidence

Keep three sources distinct: measured readers of this blog, confirmed topic questions from question/search evidence, and the user's intended audience.
A topic question establishes that the question appeared in that source; it does not establish that this blog's measured readers asked it.
Demographic shares, shopping searches and an intended audience cannot fill missing blog audience observations or manufacture interests.

For each supplied or tool-confirmed question, retain its source and scope, cite the relevant draft paragraph and identify what it actually explains.
Mark partial when the passage addresses only one component of the question; specify the remaining explanation rather than generating it.
Distinguish a supported answer, an unsupported assertion and an absent answer.
An uncertainty disclosure can be appropriate without resolving the reader's practical question.
Questions supplied as the user's hypotheses remain proposed questions, not measured demand.

With partial draft coverage, an unseen answer is unresolved rather than globally absent.
Use stable target/revision and complete returned pages before claiming whole-draft coverage.
Preserve original facts and quote only available passages; do not rewrite, impersonate readers or treat the checklist as observed reactions.
