# Bundled disclosure rules (English translation)

This is an English translation of the bundled excerpts of article 3 of the Korean Fair Labeling and Advertising Act and section 5 of the endorsement guidelines. It is a dated copy whose effective date has not been verified. Quote only the provisions below, label quotations as translated bundled text, and check current official text before acting. Do not present a translation as the original Korean wording.

## Section 0. Prohibition of unfair labeling and advertising (article 3)

> Businesses must not engage in, or cause another business to engage in, labeling or advertising that may deceive or mislead consumers and undermine fair trade: false or exaggerated advertising, deceptive advertising, unfair comparative advertising, or disparaging advertising.

The specific economic-interest disclosure guidance is in the endorsement guidelines below; do not invent a separate statutory article named disclosure duty.

## Section 1. Economic interest

> When an economic relationship between an advertiser and an endorser may affect the content or credibility of the endorsement, the advertiser or endorser must disclose that relationship.

The bundled examples include cash, products, vouchers, points, discounts, employment, group-purchase arrangements, sales commissions, and reimbursement after a review. Disclosure is made for each commercial endorsement so consumers can understand the relationship.

> Exceptionally, an economic relationship that does not affect the content or credibility of the endorsement may be left undisclosed.

The bundled exception examples are voluntary reviews after receiving a sample available to every applicant or a souvenir provided to every event participant. Do not extend these exceptions to ordinary sponsorships.

## Section 2. Clear wording

> Clearly state the nature of the economic relationship, such as monetary support, a discount, or sponsorship, in wording consumers can easily understand.

The bundled examples of clear wording explicitly state compensation, monetary support, paid advertising, a free product, product sponsorship, a product discount, or an advertising/sponsorship label. Vague wording such as trial review, used it for a week, trial group, promotional information, gift, sent by the brand, may receive a small commission, a brand hashtag alone, a collaboration label, or only a foreign-language Sponsor/AD/Collabo label is not accepted as clear in the bundled excerpt.

## Section 3. Medium and placement

- Text posts: put the disclosure in the title or first part. In the first part, use larger text or a different color. The bundled examples explicitly acknowledge compensation; disclosure hidden in the middle, only in a comment, or behind a read-more control is an unsuitable placement.
- Photos: put it in the image or, when clearly connected and recognizable, at the start of the accompanying text. A hashtag disclosure goes first in the hashtag list.
- Video: put it in the title or at the beginning and end, displaying it repeatedly during the relevant segments. The bundled example repeats a disclosure caption every five minutes. A mobile-truncated title that hides the disclosure is unsuitable.
- Live streams: follow the video guidance; if captions are difficult, repeat an audible disclosure at the beginning and end and for viewers joining later. The bundled example repeats it every five minutes; one mention in a thirty-minute stream is unsuitable.

Real estate, financial products, environmental claims, suspension orders, and fine calculations are outside these excerpts.
