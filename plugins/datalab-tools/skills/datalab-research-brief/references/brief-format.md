# Research brief format

One-line summary, Confirmed findings, Not confirmed, and optional Demand. Each confirmed fact links to the title and URL of a page actually read; if two pages support it, cite both. Unread candidates and unsupported requested facts belong in Not confirmed.

No reliability numbers or grades, relevance/verification percentages, average credibility, or quality certification. No tool measured these; citations and specific limitations communicate the evidence instead. Do not ask for unsupported region/language/period filtering options. Keep the brief proportional to the pages actually read.
