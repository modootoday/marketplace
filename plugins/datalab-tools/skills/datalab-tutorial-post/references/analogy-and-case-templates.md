# Analogy and case patterns

Choose one fitting analogy: a role in a familiar system; a journey from start to destination with obstacles; or a transformation from problem through mechanism to changed outcome. A blog category can be compared to a library classification directing a reader to a shelf.

Use verified named cases or explicitly generalized situations. Explain background, problem, application, and what changed. Do not invent people, companies, percentages, amounts, ranks, or multipliers. One analogy per post is enough; do not repeat examples merely to extend length.
