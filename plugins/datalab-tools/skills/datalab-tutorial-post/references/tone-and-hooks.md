# Tutorial tone and openings

Use a warm, respectful senior-colleague tone. Vary between a modest admission of confusion, a familiar everyday situation, and a supported surprising fact. Do not invent autobiographical facts. Shorten sentences when several new concepts arrive together; explain one step at a time without excessive encouragement.

Do not add attributed famous-person quotes, invented completion/satisfaction figures, scored self-assessments, badges, or progress bars. Replace a scored quiz with an unscored practice question. Use text subheadings.
