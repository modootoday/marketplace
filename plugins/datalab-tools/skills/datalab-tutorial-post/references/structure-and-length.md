# One-post structure and length

Complete problem, solution, and practice in one post under a 12,000-character ceiling. This is the skill's drafting budget; retries do not authorize padding or additional billed drafts.

| Part | Suggested characters | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 200 to 400 | One approachable opening |
| Problem | 1,000 to 1,800 | Visible difficulty and its underlying reason |
| Solution | 5,000 to 7,000 | Concept, one analogy, one verified or generalized case |
| Practice | 1,500 to 2,500 | One small task, two at most |
| Close | 300 to 500 | Recap and one next question |

These are flexible targets, not minimums. Trim the solution if the combined draft would exceed the ceiling. A complete shorter post is finished. If a requested minimum exceeds 12,000, explicitly explain the ceiling and offer a series. Do not claim the ceiling is a universal model limit.
