# Topic discovery

Start with platform and category. If both are missing, ask for the platform and then category. Audience and goal are optional; existing channel strengths help only when relevant. Begin research once platform and category are known.

Vary angles: how-to, list, comparison, supported experience, problem/solution, myth-busting, audience-specific, deep dive. Try a few angles against measured demand and competitors; an angle alone is not a topic.

For platforms other than Naver, label suggestions as general patterns, not measured. Seasonal calendars are hypotheses: check keyword_yoy before claiming a seasonal opportunity; do not assume movable holidays fall in a fixed month.
