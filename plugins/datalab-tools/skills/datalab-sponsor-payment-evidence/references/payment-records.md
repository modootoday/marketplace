# Payment record contract

Use stable supplied identity fields to connect agreement, invoice, acceptance and receipt. Multiple exports of the same transaction ID are one evidenced payment unless separate transaction evidence establishes otherwise. Similar dates or equal amounts alone do not establish a match.

Keep fee, expense reimbursement and noncash consideration as separate lines. Where the documents show a deduction, reconcile gross amount minus that deduction with cash while retaining the stated deduction label and any missing support. A reported withholding is not proof of final tax settlement. Preserve recoverable or unconfirmed differences as questions rather than inventing their cause.

A dated due clause and a conditional due clause have different anchors. Use only explicit acceptance evidence for an acceptance-based term, and preserve missing hour, time zone or calendar convention. Report a snapshot of evidenced receipt status separately from a sender's promise. Do not convert an unknown receipt into zero payment or a disputed balance into a legally enforceable demand.
