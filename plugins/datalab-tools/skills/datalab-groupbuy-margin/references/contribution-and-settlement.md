# Contribution and settlement

Commission example structure: contract-defined eligible sales times supplied commission rate equals creator commission revenue. Deduct only costs assigned to the creator. The relevant margin denominator is commission revenue, not the supplier's entire sales base.

Resale example structure: retained sales receipts minus the cost of units sold, creator-borne shipping, processing and other supplied variable costs, and documented unrecoverable losses equals operating contribution. The margin denominator is retained sales receipts. Purchased but recoverable unsold or returned stock is inventory/cash exposure, not automatically a realized operating loss. If recoverability or resale value is unknown, report that question rather than assuming full loss or no loss.

Reconcile payout separately: eligible amount minus identified deductions minus unpaid holds equals paid cash, when those inputs are supplied and aligned. A deduction already included in net payout must not be applied again. A hold affects cash timing unless evidence establishes an economic loss. Commission invoiced, commission earned and cash received may have different periods.

Use an input ledger identifying amount, period, scope, gross/net status, bearer and source. Preserve observed zero versus unknown. Without campaign-linked records or an agreed allocation, report store totals as supporting context and leave campaign profit unresolved. Do not manufacture tax, conversion, default refund, shipping or commission assumptions.
