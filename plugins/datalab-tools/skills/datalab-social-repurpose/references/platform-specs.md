# Caption working limits

These are the skill's standard caption targets; user-specified stricter limits take priority. They do not establish performance predictions or current account-specific feature availability.

| Platform | Character ceiling | Opening and structure | Hashtag guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X | 280 | Core message first, link last; leave link room | 1 to 3 |
| Threads | 500 | Conversational opening, context, optional question | 3 to 5 |
| Instagram | 2,200 | Finish hook and key idea within the first 125 characters, then details | 10 to 15 |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 | Hook within 140 characters, context, evidence, takeaway; blank lines between paragraphs | 3 to 5 |

Hashtag counts are optional writing guidance, not enforced technical limits. Preserve the source's tone and supplied links; with no link, use an explicit [LINK] placeholder rather than inventing a URL. LinkedIn copy uses only source-provided statistics. Use plain text labels.
