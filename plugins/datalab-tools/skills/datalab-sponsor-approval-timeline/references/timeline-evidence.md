# Timeline evidence contract

Resolve relative dates from the originating message, not today's date or a later forward. A numeric UTC offset supplied for that message permits conversion; a named zone without enough date context may require clarification. Preserve a date-only deadline as date-only rather than inventing an hour.

An agreement, a requested amendment and an accepted amendment are different evidence states. Match approval wording to the deliverable/version and its stated scope. A request to revise content does not approve the revision or waive an earlier deadline. Separate approval to use a draft from publication evidence and from authorization to execute an action.

For unresolved records, identify the exact missing anchor, time zone, calendar convention, version or approver confirmation. Ask for that information without claiming the parties agreed. Use original source IDs so the owner can inspect each interpretation.
