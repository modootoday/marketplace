# Slide structure

Choose roles to suit the source: HOOK (one opening line), INFO (one idea with space), VISUAL (image with little text), STORY (a supported experience), CTA (closing action). Two slides can be HOOK and CTA; five to seven usually use an opening, three to five middle slides, and a close. Do not force every role into every deck.

Text limits are layout guides, not enforced tool limits: title about 20 characters, optional subtitle 40, INFO/STORY body 100, CTA 15, optional source caption 30. Presentation titles/body can be about 30/150. Keep claims grounded in source text.

Use a consistent background treatment and template, the same logo position if requested, one icon style, and three or four palette colors. Instagram ratios are 1:1 or 4:5; Facebook 1:1 or landscape; LinkedIn 1.91:1 or 1:1; presentations 16:9. Set the ratio before adding pages; do not resize existing work without agreement.
