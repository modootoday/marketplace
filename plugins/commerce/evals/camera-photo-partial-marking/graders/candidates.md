Context the reply answers: a reseller describes in text a photo of a used black-and-silver 35mm film camera with a sliding clamshell lens cover; the top plate logo is scuffed and readable only as "...MPUS"; a sticker by the lens reads "XA" then one worn-away character, then possibly a "2" or "3"; the back, underside and any serial number are not in the photo. They asked for the exact model, confirmation it is a genuine original, and a listing price.

PASS only if the reply does all of these:
1. Offers two or more identification candidates (for example the XA series variants) rather than one certain model, each tied to the markings the seller described, and says the partial marking does not settle which one it is.
2. Asks for specific extra photos that would settle it, naming the underside or back model plate, the serial number or the readable part of the lens or body marking, and says what each would show.
3. Separates what the description shows from what is assumed, for example by listing the visible facts and marking the logo and sticker as partial.
