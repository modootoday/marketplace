# Worked example: short answers that stay inside the plan

Synthetic plan: EUR 90k to replace the clinic network's appointment software. Bullets: no-shows
reached 18% in the spring quarter; the new tool sends reminders and will "cut no-shows
sharply" (no figure); the vendor contract runs 24 months; staff training takes 2 weeks; 5 front-desk
staff use it.

## Allowed and forbidden in a draft answer

A draft answer may only restate a plan bullet with its figure, period and unit, or say what
must be gathered. It may not add a reason, a comparison, a location claim or a figure.

| Question | Forbidden draft (adds a claim) | Allowed draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why this vendor? | "We reviewed the market and this vendor is the best fit." | "The plan does not show another vendor. Gather the price and reminder features of at least one alternative, such as the current tool's add-on." |
| Why now? | "Peak season starts next month, so it is urgent." | "No-shows reached 18% in the spring quarter. Gather the no-show rate for earlier quarters to show whether it is rising." |
| What do we get for EUR 90k? | "It covers licences, setup and support for two years." | "The plan gives EUR 90k, a 24-month contract and 2 weeks of training, with no breakdown. Gather an itemised cost and the cost per missed appointment." |
| How much will no-shows fall? | "They should drop to about 9%." | "The plan says no-shows will fall sharply, with no figure. Gather the no-show rates at clinics that already use reminders to size it." |

## Rules the table shows

- If a question has no support in the plan, the answer is "The plan does not show this" plus
  what to gather. Do not write a sentence that sounds like an answer.
- Never write "we reviewed", "we chose because", "better than", "cheaper than" unless a bullet
  says it.
- Use placeholders such as X only in a to-gather note, never as a number inside an answer.
- Close with the three most likely to hurt questions and what to bring.

## Check before sending

Read each draft answer and underline every claim. Each underlined claim must match a plan
bullet word for word in figure, period and unit; if it does not, replace it with a gather
note.
