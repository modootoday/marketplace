We ran an anonymous pulse survey in a 40-person company and got 15 free-text answers to "What one thing would make work better?". Turn them into themes and an action plan for the leadership meeting on Friday.

R1: "Too many status meetings. Half could be an email."
R2: "I never know who decides things, requests just disappear."
R3: "Standups run 40 minutes. Please cut them."
R4: "The laptop I got is slow, builds take ages."
R5: "Decisions are made in DMs and I find out a week later."
R6: "Meetings with no agenda."
R7: "Would love a clearer promotion path."
R8: "Nobody tells us why priorities change."
R9: "Our desks are too close to the kitchen, loud."
R10: "Too many meetings, I only get focus time after 5pm."
R11: "One manager shouts at people in front of the team and nothing happens when it is raised."
R12: "Wifi drops in the east meeting room."
R13: "Hard to know who to ask for approval, it bounces between three people."
R14: "The coffee machine is always broken."
R15: "Meetings, meetings, meetings."
