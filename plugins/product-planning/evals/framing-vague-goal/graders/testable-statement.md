PASS only if all three hold:
1. The problem statement names a specific user (for example a new workspace admin or an invited
   member) and a specific point where they get stuck, rather than "onboarding is bad".
2. It names one measurable signal with a direction (for example the share of invited members who
   send a first message within a week should rise), and says that the baseline value is unknown or
   must be measured if it was not given.
3. It labels its own assumptions as assumptions (words such as "assuming", "hypothesis", or a
   section of open questions).

FAIL if the statement stays generic, has no measurable signal, or presents guesses as facts.
