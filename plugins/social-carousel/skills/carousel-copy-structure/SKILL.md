# Carousel copy

A carousel is read one thumb-swipe at a time. Each slide has to earn the next
swipe, and the first slide competes with everything else in the feed. A Korean
example is in `references/example.ko.md`.

## The arc

1. **Hook (slide 1).** One promise or one surprising fact that the rest pays
   off. Specific beats clever: a number, a named situation, the reader's own
   words. No "you won't believe".
2. **Problem (1-2 slides).** The situation the reader recognises, in their
   words.
3. **Value (2-5 slides).** One idea per slide: a step, a tip, a before and
   after. If a slide needs two ideas, it is two slides.
4. **Summary (1 slide).** The whole carousel in one sentence or a short list,
   for people who swiped fast.
5. **Call to action (last slide).** One action only (save, follow, visit, reply
   with a word). Several asks get none.

## Line rules

- A headline per slide that makes sense alone, then at most two short lines of
  support. If it needs a paragraph, it belongs in the caption.
- Numbers as numerals, units included.
- The same register throughout (in Korean, the polite -yo form for a brand
  speaking to customers).
- No claim the brand cannot back: "best", "only", guaranteed results.

## Output

A table: slide number, role (hook, problem, value, summary, CTA), headline,
support lines. Then a caption that adds what did not fit, and three alternative
hooks for testing.
