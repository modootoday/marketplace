PASS only if all three hold:
1. The slides follow a hook, a problem, value slides with one idea each, a summary slide, and a
   call to action, with a short headline per slide.
2. The last slide asks for one action only, and the reply explains why it narrowed the three
   requested asks (follow, visit, buy) to one, or moves the others to the caption.
3. The copy is in Korean in one consistent polite register (-yo form).

FAIL if any of the three is missing, or if the final slide carries all three asks.
