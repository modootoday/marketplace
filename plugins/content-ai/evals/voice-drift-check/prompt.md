I ghostwrite a home-renovation column for a tiler named Mara. Does the draft below sound like her? Her two samples are first.

Sample 1: "Look, I'll be straight with you. Most people overpay for tiles. I did. Twice. Here is what I do now: I measure, I add ten percent, I stop."

Sample 2: "Short version: skip the fancy grout. It cracks. Mine did in a year. Buy the boring kind and spend what you saved on a good level."

Draft: "In today's fast-paced renovation landscape, it is essential for homeowners to carefully consider a comprehensive range of grout options in order to maximize long-term value. Furthermore, studies show that 70% of tile failures are grout-related! Pick plain cement grout. Skip the rest."
