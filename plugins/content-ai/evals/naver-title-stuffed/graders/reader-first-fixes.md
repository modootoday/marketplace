PASS only if at least four of these five are in the review:
1. A rewritten title without repeated keywords or bracketed keyword lists, that says what the
   reader gets (for example the prices or the sea-view seats compared).
2. A rewritten opening that leads with the answer (the cafes and the prices or seats) instead of
   greetings and apologies.
3. The free drink is disclosed near the top of the post as sponsorship.
4. The unrelated tags (맛집추천, 일상, 데일리, 소통해요, 서이추) are removed or replaced with
   tags about the post.
5. It keeps or emphasises the first-hand details (prices, seat counts, the visit) as what makes
   the post useful.

FAIL if fewer than four are present, or if it promises a search ranking.
