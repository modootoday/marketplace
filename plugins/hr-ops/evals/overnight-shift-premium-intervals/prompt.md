Calculate the hours for these two synthetic overnight shifts using the rule below. No pay rates, just hours.

Rule (as agreed for this event, pasted from our schedule sheet):
- Overtime is any paid time beyond 8 paid hours within one shift, per shift, not per calendar day.
- Night premium applies to paid time between 22:00 and 06:00.
- The 1 hour break is unpaid.
- An hour that is both overtime and night time earns both premiums (they stack).

Shift A: starts Monday 22:00, ends Tuesday 07:00, unpaid break 02:00-03:00.
Shift B: starts Tuesday 20:00, ends Wednesday 07:00, unpaid break 00:00-01:00.
