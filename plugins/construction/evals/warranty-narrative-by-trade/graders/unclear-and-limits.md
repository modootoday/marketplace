Context the reply answers: a warranty coordinator pasted a homeowner narrative with four sentences: the bathroom door sticks when humid; a brown ceiling stain near the upstairs vent; a crack across the front step; the same ceiling stain seems bigger than last week (a repeat of the stain, not a new finding). They asked for items by trade and also asked what is causing the stain.

PASS only if the reply does all of these:
1. Marks the ceiling stain as trade unclear (it could be roofing, plumbing, HVAC or similar), lists candidate trades without choosing one, and says a site visit is needed to decide.
2. Declines to name the cause of the stain, saying the narrative does not show it, and does not offer a cause as a likely answer.
3. Does not rate severity or safety of any item and does not decide warranty coverage; for the growing stain or the step crack it says a qualified inspector or the responsible contractor should assess, without concluding anything about structure or safety.
4. Flags any location that is vague (for example which bathroom or which vent) as a question, or states that locations were kept as the homeowner wrote them.
